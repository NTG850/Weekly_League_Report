🚨 WEEK 10 TRADE DEADLINE RECAP

THE LEAGUE OF LEGENDS TRADE MARKET WENT CRAZY!

1. Tristan & Brady — A Little Help at WR

Tristan sends Malik Washington to Brady in exchange for Dalton Kincaid.

Brady was looking for some much-needed wide receiver depth, while Tristan gets to pair Josh Allen with a familiar target in Kincaid. A move that makes sense for both sides.

2. Dalton & Erick — THE MONDAY NIGHT BLOCKBUSTER

Dalton sends Omarion Hampton and Ladd McConkey to Erick for Tuten.

Dalton was looking for a dependable RB2 to pair with Gibbs. With Barkley and Jeanty bringing some inconsistency, Tuten could be exactly what this team needed.

Erick, meanwhile, desperately needed wide receiver help. And after watching Hampton go off Monday night, he’s probably feeling pretty good about this one. Did Erick just pull off a masterclass?

3. Dalton & Luke — A SUNDAY MORNING SURPRISE

After four or five days of back-and-forth negotiations, Dalton and Luke finally got a deal done just before kickoff Sunday morning.

Dalton sends Jameson Williams, Tucker Kraft, and young prospect Cyrus Allen in exchange for Parker Washington and Kyle Monangai.

A massive shake-up for both rosters. These two clearly weren’t afraid to make a move before the deadline.

4. Austin & Nathan — THE BIGGEST BLOCKBUSTER OF THEM ALL

Nathan adds Bucky Irving and Davante Adams as he makes a push for the playoffs.

Austin goes younger at wide receiver, bringing in Chris Olave and Pat Bryant, while also adding Ollie Gordon for this season.

This was the biggest trade of the deadline by far. Both teams are betting on a different path to success. Only time will tell who comes out on top.

5. Nathan & Tristan — A FUTURE-FOCUSED SWAP

Another shocking move earlier in the week!

Tristan moves on from veteran Josh Jacobs and Jordan Addison, bringing in a younger running back group with Quinshon Judkins and Dylan Sampson, while also adding Stefon Diggs to help put points on the board each week.

Nathan gets the veteran pieces in Jacobs and Addison. Tristan gets younger at running back while still adding help for the present.
